# Dhara

Dhara answers a plain-Bangla citizen question with the exact law provision that answers it, plus its official citation. The vocabulary below exists because several of these words carry two or three meanings in ordinary use, and each one is also a field name in the frozen chunk schema.

## The law

**Provision**:
The umbrella unit of law that Dhara retrieves and cites — a ধারা in an Act, an অনুচ্ছেদ in the Constitution. Use it whenever a statement is true of both.
_Avoid_: Clause, rule, law (when a single numbered unit is meant)

**Section**:
A ধারা specifically — a numbered provision of an Act. Not a Constitution article, not a part of the report, not a `§` of the implementation guide.
_Avoid_: Article, clause

**Article**:
An অনুচ্ছেদ of the Constitution specifically. The Constitution has articles; Acts have sections. Both are provisions.
_Avoid_: Section (when the Constitution is meant)

**Act**:
A single enacted law with its own page on the bdlaws portal, identified by that portal's act id.
_Avoid_: Law, statute, ordinance (an ordinance is one kind of Act and is named as such only when the distinction matters)

**Sub-section**:
A numbered subdivision inside one section — `(১)`, `(২)`. It stays with its parent section and is never retrieved or cited on its own.
_Avoid_: Clause, part

## Retrieval units

**Chunk**:
The unit that gets embedded, indexed, and scored. Usually one provision; a long provision is split into several chunks that share one citation.
_Avoid_: Passage, document, segment

**Sub-chunk**:
A chunk that is one piece of a split provision, distinguished by `sub_idx`. Sub-chunks are deduplicated back to their provision before anything is shown to a user.
_Avoid_: Fragment, window

**Corpus**:
The frozen set of chunks a given experiment ran against, always named with its version (`corpus_v1`). A corpus without a version number is not a thing that exists.
_Avoid_: Dataset, index, database

## Questions and evaluation

**Gold question**:
A real citizen question hand-mapped by a human to its correct provisions. Gold questions are evaluated against and never trained on.
_Avoid_: Test question, eval example

**Synthetic question**:
A question generated by a language model from a provision it is meant to answer. Training only, never evaluation.
_Avoid_: Generated query, augmented data

**Mined question**:
A real citizen question collected from a public source and stripped of identifying detail. Contributes to both training and the gold pool.
_Avoid_: Scraped question, harvested query

**Register**:
Whether a question is phrased colloquially or formally. The tag that carries the project's central claim, assigned from the vocabulary a question actually uses, not from who wrote it.
_Avoid_: Style, tone, formality

**Domain**:
The area of law a provision or question belongs to. Confirmed values: `family`, `land`, `labour`, `consumer`, `cybercrime`, `constitutional` — the set is being expanded toward the law civilians meet most often, and the authoritative list lives in `configs/domains.yaml`. Always this sense. The machine-learning sense is written out in full as "domain-specific fine-tuning" and never appears as a bare noun.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, area, intent

**Intent**:
Not a separate concept in Dhara. The classifier predicts `domain`; say so. A second name for one label costs a paragraph of confusion in the report.
_Avoid_: Use `domain` instead

**Rung**:
One of the five retrieval configurations on the experimental ladder. Every rung is a reported result, never a discarded prototype.
_Avoid_: Model, baseline (when a specific rung is meant), approach

**Risk tier**:
How much damage a wrong retrieval does in a given domain — high, medium, low. A property of the domain, not of retrieval difficulty. It changes how an answer is framed and where the abstention threshold sits; it never changes ranking.
_Avoid_: Severity, priority, sensitivity

**Verbatim text**:
A mined question exactly as its source published it. Kept locally, never committed, never released — the source is copyrighted.
_Avoid_: Original question, source text

**Paraphrase**:
The rewritten form of a mined question that preserves its register while changing its wording. The only form that enters the released dataset.
_Avoid_: Cleaned question, rewrite, normalized question

**Unanswerable question**:
A gold question with no correct provision in the corpus, included deliberately at 10–15% of the set so the abstention threshold can be calibrated against something real.
_Avoid_: Negative example, out-of-scope query, distractor

**Annotation mode**:
Whether a gold question was answered from a machine-generated candidate list (`assisted`) or with no list at all (`unaided`). Recorded per question because the assisted mode biases the set toward what a lexical retriever can find.
_Avoid_: Labelling method, annotation type

**Abstention**:
The system declining to answer because no candidate cleared the calibrated score threshold. A deliberate outcome, not a failure.
_Avoid_: No result, empty response, fallback

## Text handling

**Light normalization**:
The normalization applied to text before a transformer sees it — NFC, ZWNJ strip, whitespace. Anything more moves the input off the distribution the tokenizer learned.
_Avoid_: Basic cleaning, preprocessing

**Aggressive normalization**:
The normalization applied to text before a lexical model sees it — light, plus ZWJ strip, Bangla-to-ASCII digits, punctuation padding, lowercasing.
_Avoid_: Full cleaning, deep normalization

**Raw text**:
A provision exactly as the portal printed it, held in `text_raw`. The only text a Bangla reader is ever shown.
_Avoid_: Original text, source text

**Citation**:
The metadata that lets a reader find a provision in the published law — Act name, provision number, chapter, source URL, crawl date. An answer without one is not an answer.
_Avoid_: Reference, source, metadata (when the citation specifically is meant)
